# Project Requirement Document (PRD)

# CODO AI Innovations – Corporate Digital Platform

## 1. Strategic Overview

### 1.1 Corporate Identity and Positioning

CODO AI Innovations represents the central strategic entity governing a dual-vertical ecosystem composed of:

* **CODO Academy** – Education & professional training division
* **CODO Agency** – Custom digital solutions and technology services division

The corporate website must function as the authoritative digital headquarters of the brand, articulating vision, credibility, and technological leadership. It is not merely an informational website; it is a strategic brand infrastructure designed to:

* Establish digital authority
* Demonstrate ecosystem cohesion
* Drive enterprise-level lead acquisition
* Reinforce long-term brand equity

The platform must communicate global readiness, technological depth, and institutional maturity.

## 2. Purpose & Strategic Objectives

### 2.1 Core Vision

To position CODO AI Innovations as a premium, AI-driven technology organization capable of delivering enterprise-grade digital solutions while fostering future-ready talent.

### 2.2 Primary Goals

* Establish a strong corporate brand presence.
* Clearly communicate the ecosystem structure:
  * Parent Company → Academy + Agency
* Generate qualified business leads for CODO Agency.
* Build institutional trust through portfolio, leadership visibility, and client associations.
* Enable long-term content growth through structured blogging and thought leadership.

## 3. Target Audience Architecture

The website must address multiple audience segments simultaneously:

### 3.1 Business & Enterprise Clients

* Startups seeking digital product development
* SMEs requiring automation or AI solutions
* Enterprises looking for custom software engineering

### 3.2 Academic & Student Audience

* Students exploring training programs
* Interns seeking skill development pathways

### 3.3 Professional Network

* Hiring partners
* Industry collaborators
* Strategic investors

Content hierarchy must guide each segment through tailored pathways without causing navigational friction.

## 4. Brand & Visual Governance Framework

### 4.1 Brand Compliance

The website must strictly adhere to the official CODO AI Innovations Brand Kit.

* Approved color palette only
* Official typography only
* Defined spacing and visual rhythm
* Brand-consistent UI components
* No deviation in visual identity

Uniformity across all pages is mandatory.

### 4.2 Design Philosophy

The digital experience must embody:

* Premium minimalism
* Structured typography hierarchy
* Generous white space
* High-quality imagery
* Subtle motion design and micro-interactions

The aesthetic direction should balance corporate authority with technological innovation.

### 4.3 Theme Architecture

The platform must support:

* Dark Mode
* Light Mode
* User toggle functionality
* System preference detection

Each mode must maintain:

* WCAG-compliant contrast ratios
* Brand-approved color variations
* Consistent component behavior

## 5. UX & Interaction Principles

The user experience must prioritize:

* Clear information architecture
* Logical content flow
* Minimal cognitive load
* Fast loading speeds
* Fully responsive mobile-first structure

Animations must be smooth but purposeful—never decorative without intent.

The CODO AI Innovations website is not a marketing artifact; it is a digital corporate headquarters.
